# SEO, AEO and GEO operations

## Implemented

- Canonical origin and metadata title/description.
- Open Graph and X/Twitter large-image cards.
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml generated by Next.js metadata routes.
- Semantic page sections, descriptive heading hierarchy, event facts and visible FAQs.
- JSON-LD entities: WebSite, Event and FAQPage.
- Local context: Mysuru, Karnataka, India; en-IN; IN-KA; venue represented as a Schema.org Place / PostalAddress.
- Organizer identity aligned to the brochure: Innovators & Visionaries Club (IVC), VVCE, in collaboration with inunity.
- The ₹999 amount is described as payable only by shortlisted teams after selection, not as a general registration fee.
- Tentative and unspecified details are explicitly labeled; no registration platform URL is fabricated.

## Release checklist

1. Keep the canonical domain in NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL and src/app/sitemap.ts / src/app/robots.ts aligned.
2. Check page title, description, canonical, OG image, robots and sitemap on production.
3. Validate JSON-LD with Schema.org Validator and Google's Rich Results Test.
4. Add the domain to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit /sitemap.xml.
5. Verify organizer, event date, venue, team size, selection, fee, domains and prize pool against the current brochure before changing copy or structured data.
6. Add a registration URL only after organizers publish and verify it. Never mark a placeholder as an active offer.
7. Keep meaningful images accessible; decorative full-bleed backgrounds remain hidden from assistive technology.

## AEO / GEO content rules

- Lead with direct answers: event identity, organizer, dates, venue, team size, registration window, selection, fee and prizes.
- Keep FAQ visible in page content and synchronized with FAQ JSON-LD.
- Use stable entity names: SUSTAINIQ 2026, Innovators & Visionaries Club (IVC), VVCE, inunity and Mysuru, Karnataka, India.
- Preserve uncertainty markers: PPT screening on 25/26 November is tentative; results/closing timing by 1 PM on 4 December is to be finalized.
- Do not invent registration platform links, sponsor claims, social proof or availability.

Search rankings, AI citations, answer-engine visibility and rich-result display cannot be guaranteed by metadata or schema alone.
