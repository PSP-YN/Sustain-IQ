# SEO, AEO and GEO operations

## Implemented

- Canonical origin and metadata title/description.
- Open Graph and X/Twitter large-image cards.
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml generated by Next.js metadata routes.
- Semantic HTML sections, descriptive heading hierarchy, explicit event details and visible FAQs.
- JSON-LD entities: WebSite, Event and FAQPage.
- Local context: Mysuru, Karnataka, India; en-IN; IN-KA; venue represented as a Schema.org Place / PostalAddress.
- Conservative factual copy: unconfirmed registration platform, domains and schedule remain clearly marked as pending.

## Release checklist

1. Keep the canonical domain in NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL and src/app/sitemap.ts / src/app/robots.ts aligned.
2. Check page title, description, canonical, OG image, robots and sitemap on production.
3. Validate JSON-LD with Schema.org Validator and Google's Rich Results Test. Google may not display Event rich results unless all eligibility requirements are met.
4. Add the domain to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit /sitemap.xml.
5. Verify event name, date, venue, team size, registration price and prize pool against organiser-approved source material before changing copy or structured data.
6. Add the confirmed registration URL only after organisers publish it. Never mark a placeholder as an active offer or invent availability.
7. Keep meaningful images accessible; decorative full-bleed backgrounds remain hidden from assistive technology.

## AEO / GEO content rules

- Lead with direct answers: what the event is, who can join, when/where it occurs, team size and fee.
- Keep FAQ visible in page content and synchronized with FAQ JSON-LD.
- Use stable entity names: SUSTAINIQ 2026, Civil Department Association, VVCE, Mysuru, Karnataka, India.
- Maintain consistent facts on official social profiles and registration pages once confirmed.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, fabricated social proof, fake testimonials, fake links and unsupported claims.

Search rankings, AI citations, answer-engine visibility and rich-result display cannot be guaranteed by metadata or schema alone.
